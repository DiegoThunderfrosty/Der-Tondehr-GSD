# Changelog

## 0.1.4

- Renamed the public product to Der Tondehr GSD and the manufacturer to Der Tondehr.
- Kept the historical processor class and plugin identifiers for session compatibility.
- Improved full-scale and above-full-scale stability so legal floating-point peaks remain processed rather than producing an unintended dry or frozen result.
- Preserved deterministic recovery after bypass and host activation changes.
- Added public repository documentation, dependency pinning, and reproducible Windows build scripts.

## 0.1.3

- Refined the secondary harmonic family and high-drive calibration.
- Improved continuity around extreme peaks.

## 0.1.2

- Corrected the trapezoidal nonlinear-feedback solver.
- Added safeguarded convergence behavior for discontinuities and damaged state.

## 0.1.1

- Corrected the upper-harmonic decay and premature high-frequency collapse.
- Replaced the earlier audible decimation roll-off with a higher-order anti-alias response.

Private comparison material and development-session history are not included in the public repository.
